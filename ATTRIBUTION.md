# Attribution

**Overhead for Tesserae** by [dmellok](https://github.com/dmellok), [tesserae-widget-overhead](https://github.com/dmellok/tesserae-widget-overhead), AGPL-3.0-or-later. This Recipe ports its Photo and Plate pages: the aircraft type table (ICAO designator → name, class, wingspan), the operator table (callsign prefix → IATA code, name), the row logic (bearing, trend, class guessing from category and prefix), the photo and route lookups, and the page layout. The silhouettes drawn for the widget are MIT and are inlined in `src/full.liquid`.

**Positions:** [adsb.lol](https://adsb.lol), a community ADS-B aggregator.

**Routes:** [adsbdb](https://www.adsbdb.com).

**Photographs:** [planespotters.net](https://www.planespotters.net/photo/api), each credited to its photographer on the screen; thumbnails are loaded by the rendering device and never stored.

**Framework:** the [TRMNL framework](https://trmnl.com) CSS and fonts the screen is rendered with.
